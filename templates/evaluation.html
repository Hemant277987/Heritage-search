Limitations

  • Small, curated corpus (about 70 short texts, 6 categories). Scores are indicative, not generalisable.
  • Lexical matching only. Synonyms and paraphrases (“birdwatching” vs “migratory birds”) are missed, and generic words (community, royal, folk) pull in wrong categories.
  • Relevance is category-level. A “sweets” query returning Bengali Sweets (Cuisine) is counted wrong for a Festivals judgement, so P@5 understates real usefulness in overlapping topics.
  • Queries were written by the project author, who knew the corpus, which flatters results. Independent users would score lower.
  • LLM summaries (optional) are constrained to retrieved passages but can still misstate them; the extractive fallback only reuses original sentences.
